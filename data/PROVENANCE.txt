INTERPRETATION
One row describes a listing, not a booking, guest or host. Price is the reported
listing price, not realized revenue or a verified total for your travel dates.
Prices and availability can change; these are historical snapshot data.
Minimum-night rules alone do not establish availability for a seven-night trip.
Missing review dates do not imply zero quality, occupancy or demand.
The simulation treats the filtered file as a finite teaching population;
it does not establish representativeness of Boston's lodging market.
