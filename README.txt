STT303 PROJECT 2 | BOSTON ON A BUDGET | AIRBNB TEAM EDITION

1. Extract the entire ZIP and open the .Rproj file in RStudio.
2. Read Project_Instructions.html. Teams contain 2 or 3 members.
3. Run source("setup.R") if packages are missing (R 4.1+ recommended).
4. Complete analysis.R and project2.Rmd. Click Knit to produce project2.html.
5. Complete the contribution/verification section; all members approve.
6. One member submits STT303_Project2_TeamXX.zip with the entire project folder,
   including the knitted HTML. Replace XX with your team ID.

Use relative paths and keep the original CSV unchanged. The downloaded data are
already included; students need no accounts, scraping, or live data connection.
RStudio includes Pandoc for HTML reports. Package installation needs internet.
The starter report knits with prompts; replace them with your completed work.
All members contribute R analysis, interpretation and review. A member other
than the final integrator checks the project from a fresh R session.
Read data/PROVENANCE.txt for real-data attribution, preparation and limitations.
